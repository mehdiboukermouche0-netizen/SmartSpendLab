Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

SmartSpendLab (“we”, “us”) respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it and the choices you have, including your rights under the EU and UK GDPR and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA).

Information we collect

  • Calculator inputs. Numbers you enter into our calculators are processed in your browser. They are not sent to our servers and we cannot see them.
  • Preferences. Your language and currency choices are saved in your browser’s local storage so we can remember them. They stay on your device.
  • Messages. If you email us, we receive your name, email address and message, and we use them only to reply.
  • Technical data. Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs, such as IP address, browser type and pages requested, for security and reliability.

Cookies and advertising

We use Google AdSense to show ads. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user’s prior visits to this and other websites. Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to our site and other sites on the internet. You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings or aboutads.info. You can also read how Google uses information from sites that use its services. You can control cookies in your browser settings. Where required, such as in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, we ask for your consent before personalised ads are shown.

How we use information

We use information to operate and secure the site, reply to your messages, understand how the site is used and show advertising. We do not sell your personal information for money.

Your GDPR rights (EU and UK)

If you are in the EU or UK, you may ask to access, correct, delete, restrict or port your personal data, object to processing and withdraw consent at any time. Our legal bases are consent, legitimate interests such as keeping the site secure, and legal obligations. You may also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

Your CCPA rights (California)

California residents may request to know what personal information we collect, request deletion, and opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information. We do not discriminate against people who exercise these rights.

Retention

We keep emails for as long as needed to respond and for a reasonable period afterwards. Local preferences stay in your browser until you clear them.

Children

This site is not directed to children under 13 (or 16 where local law requires), and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email hello@smartspendlab.com.
