How our calculators work

We build tools to answer one question each, and we keep the inputs simple. You choose a currency, enter your numbers and see the result update instantly. Nothing is submitted to a server, so your personal figures stay on your device. Only your language and currency choices are remembered in your browser to save you from picking them again.

Calculators are estimates. They rely on the assumptions you enter, such as a steady interest rate, and they leave out things like taxes, fees and inflation. Each tool page explains what is included and what is not. For the reasoning behind the numbers, pair a calculator with the matching guide on our articles page.

Which tool should I start with?

  • If you are not sure where your money goes, start with the 50/30/20 budget calculator.
  • If you want to see what regular saving can become, try the compound interest calculator.
  • If you owe money on cards or loans, use the debt payoff planner to compare the avalanche and snowball methods.
