About SmartSpendLab

SmartSpendLab builds free tools and plain-English guides that help people take control of their everyday money. We focus on the decisions that most households face: how to split an income, how to save, how to pay off debt and how small, regular amounts grow over time.

What we do

We publish two kinds of content. Calculators let you test your own numbers privately in the browser. Guides explain the reasoning behind the numbers, with worked examples, common mistakes and next steps. Each calculator links to the guides that explain it, and each guide links to the tools it relies on.

How we create content

  • We start from a real, common question about money.
  • We explain it in plain language and show the arithmetic so you can check it.
  • We state assumptions clearly and say where rules differ between countries.
  • We avoid promises about returns and avoid recommending specific financial products.
  • We review and update pages when we find mistakes. If you spot one, please tell us.

Editorial independence

Content is written independently. Advertisers and partners do not decide what we write or recommend.

How the site is funded

SmartSpendLab is free to use. It is supported by advertising and may include affiliate links in the future. If we earn a commission from a link, we will label it clearly. See our affiliate disclosure.

Privacy

The numbers you type into our calculators are processed in your browser and are not sent to us. Read our Privacy Policy for details about cookies, advertising and your rights.
